The brand rules themselves live in `packages/brand` and at https://www.jscebu.org/brand.md. This file covers who the site is for and how it should feel.

## Audience

- **Developers in Cebu:** students, juniors and working devs who come to meetups, workshops and Hacktoberfest Cebu.
- **Volunteers, co-leads and partner communities:** they make things for JS Cebu.
- **AI agents:** they make on-brand graphics and pages, given only the brand link.

## Use cases

- Find the next event. For now that means Cebby and Facebook.
- See that the community is real and active: real photos, real projects.
- Find where to join.
- Get the logos and brand rules.

## Personality

- **The website uses the Terminal personality:** ink background, a faint grid, yellow light.
- **The two signatures carry recognition:** the block cursor at the end of the primary headline, and the logo as a tilted die-cut sticker.
- **Voice:**
  - English first, friendly and direct, like a fellow developer.
  - Bisaya only lightly, and only approved phrases.
- **Photos:** real and never filtered.
- **No hexagon motifs** beyond the logo itself.

## Motion

Added 2026-10-28, because Dorell said "a little bit of animations. right now, it's zero".

- **A little, and purposeful.** Motion follows the brand's two signatures: text arriving like terminal output, and the sticker being slapped on.
- **Easing and timing:** decelerating curves (ease-out-expo or quart), never bounce. Entrances run 400–700ms; feedback runs 100–250ms. Animate only transform and opacity.
- **One hero moment per page,** plus quiet reveals as sections scroll in and small hover and press feedback on things you can click.
- **Under `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`, nothing moves,** and the cursor doesn't blink. Content never depends on JavaScript to become visible.
