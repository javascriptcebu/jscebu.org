## Our mission

JavaScript Cebu aims to empower and unite developers by providing a collaborative platform for learning, sharing, and innovation in JavaScript and related technologies. We strive to enhance the technological landscape of Cebu through community-driven events such as meetups, codecamps, and other educational gatherings.

## Our vision

To be the leading catalyst for technological advancement and community collaboration in Cebu, fostering a vibrant ecosystem where developers can grow, innovate, and contribute to the global tech scene.

## How we measure success

We use the following metrics time to time to see how we're doing:

- **Growth in Membership:** Increasing the number of active participants and registered members attending our events.
- **Event Frequency and Diversity:** Successfully hosting a variety of events regularly, including meetups, codecamps, and special tech talks.
- **Community Engagement:** High levels of engagement in community discussions, projects, and collaborations as evidenced by feedback, social media interactions, and community-driven projects.
- **Partnerships and Sponsorships:** Establishing strong partnerships with local and international tech companies and securing sponsorships that support the sustainability and expansion of our events.
- **Success Stories:** Documenting and showcasing success stories of members who have advanced in their careers or launched successful projects as a result of our community support.
- **Positive Remarks:** We always aim to be the type of organization for the people and by the people.

## History

JavaScript Cebu's GitHub organization was created in December 2018, and the community launched in 2019, sprouting from Laravel Cebu and Vue.js Cebu. For years it was home to three sub-communities, Angular Cebu, React Cebu and Vue.js Cebu, which ran their own meetups and workshops under the JS Cebu banner. Today JS Cebu runs as one community for everyone building with JavaScript in Cebu, and it co-presents Hacktoberfest Cebu every October.
